Before you start

  1. 01

    A download key

    Issued with your licence. Trial and academic keys are sent by email once the request below is processed.

  2. 02

    A Windows machine

    ProCue ships as a Windows build. Scenarios are plain CSV and workspaces are Python modules, so nothing else needs installing to get started.

  3. 03

    Simulink, if you bring your own algorithm

    Only needed to compile your own motion cueing algorithms. The NeoClassical family ships with ProCue.

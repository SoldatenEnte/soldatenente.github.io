About

We read the papers, so you don't have to.

ProCue is made by BrownSim, an engineering services provider whose mission is to help companies maximise the returns from their simulation and digital twin activities.

01 The team

A small, senior team.

  • Dr Craig Brown

    Founder and director

    A simulation and algorithms veteran with over thirteen years of experience. Responsible for motion and cueing development on the McLaren-MTS Vehicle Dynamics Simulator, then a principal engineer at Toyota and a founding member of Toyota Gazoo Racing Simulation UK. He holds a doctorate in derivative-free optimisation, the solver at the centre of ProCue.

02 Who we are

Simulation, algorithms and the software around them.

BrownSim is a small, senior engineering practice. ProCue is our own product, built on the same published research.

03 Consulting

Work with us directly.

Motion cueing, vehicle dynamics simulation, real-time systems and the algorithms behind them. If a problem sits between the vehicle model and the hardware, it is probably one we have met before.

Consulting

Direct engineering support from Dr Brown: cueing development, simulator commissioning and shakedown, algorithm design, and the studies that inform them. Available on a retained or a per-project basis.
